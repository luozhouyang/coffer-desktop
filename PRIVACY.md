# Coffer Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Coffer is a local-first secrets manager. Your data stays on your Mac.

## What Coffer stores

Coffer stores the vault you create — secrets, project names, tags, and settings — in an encrypted SQLCipher database on your device. Your master password is never stored and is required to decrypt the vault.

## What Coffer collects

Nothing. Coffer does not collect, transmit, or share personal data. The Mac App Store version has no analytics, telemetry, advertising, crash reporting, accounts, or network access.

Other distribution channels may check for updates using GitHub Releases. The Mac App Store version does not make network requests.

## Touch ID

If you enable Touch ID, macOS performs the biometric check. Coffer never receives or stores biometric data.

## Backups

Encrypted backup files are written only to the location you choose in the save panel. You control where they go.

## Diagnostics

Coffer does not send crash logs or diagnostics to the developer.
